Modeling Emerging Transportation with Robust Optimization (METRO)

We employ optimization to study emerging mobility systems. Our research investigates how new vehicle technologies and platform services transform transportation systems, and how their benefits can be maximized for all stakeholders while adverse impacts are minimized.

Research

New vehicle technologies and emerging mobility services are reshaping how people and goods move. Our research examines this transition through three principal impact areas.

Infrastructure Adaptation

As vehicles become automated, connected, and electrified, the supporting infrastructure must evolve accordingly. Our research examines what cities should build, how such infrastructure should be priced and financed, and how these systems perform under stress.

Shared Economy

Carsharing, ride-sourcing, and on-demand delivery are among the most consequential mobility services of the past decade. Our research analyzes how the design of these platforms determines whether their benefits accrue to riders, drivers, and suppliers, or to the platform alone.

Logistics and Urban Delivery

E-commerce and on-demand services have made urban freight one of the most challenging problems facing modern cities. Our research designs delivery systems that move more goods at lower cost.

All three areas share a common methodological toolkit: equilibrium analysis, mixed-integer optimization, dynamic models, and game-theoretic market design.
